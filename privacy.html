Privacy Policy

We believe in being transparent about how we handle your information. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Last updated: March 1, 2025

Almond Ember Kitchen ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website www.almondemberkitchen.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our Site or interact with us.

By using the Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

📌 The short version: We collect minimal data, we never sell your personal information, and we use cookies mainly to understand what recipes people love. You can contact us at hello@almondemberkitchen.com to ask about or delete your data.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide voluntarily

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Contact us — name, email address, subject, and message content through our contact form.
  • Subscribe to our newsletter — name and email address.
  • Leave a comment — name, email, and comment content (if comments are enabled).
  • Participate in surveys or giveaways — any information you choose to provide.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, certain information is collected automatically, including:

  • Log data — IP address, browser type, operating system, referring pages, pages visited, and timestamps.
  • Device data — device type, screen size, and general location (country/city level).
  • Cookies and similar technologies — see Section 5 below and our Cookies Policy for full details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries, questions, and requests.
  • To send you our newsletter if you have opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To improve the content, recipes, and user experience of the Site.
  • To monitor and analyze usage trends and preferences.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or security incidents.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Consent — when you submit a contact form or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Legitimate interests — to improve our Site, prevent fraud, and ensure security.
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to process data by law.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers — trusted third parties who help us operate the Site (e.g., email delivery, analytics). These providers are contractually bound to protect your data.
  • Legal authorities — if required by law, subpoena, or to protect our rights, property, or safety.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case your data may be transferred as part of the business.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. For detailed information on the cookies we use and how to manage them, please see our Cookies Policy.

In short, we use:

  • Essential cookies — necessary for the Site to function.
  • Analytics cookies — to understand how visitors use the Site (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Preference cookies — to remember your settings and choices.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, some parts of the Site may not function properly without them.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Contact form submissions — retained for up to 12 months after resolution, then deleted.
  • Newsletter subscriptions — retained until you unsubscribe, plus a short period for record-keeping.
  • Analytics data — retained in accordance with our analytics provider's settings (typically 26 months).

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restriction — request that we restrict processing of your data.
  • Portability — request a transfer of your data to another service.
  • Objection — object to our processing of your data based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@almondemberkitchen.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission.
  • Regular security reviews of our systems and service providers.
  • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Third-Party Links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., social media platforms, recipe sources). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any site you visit.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. International Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We take steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this policy.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us:

  • Email: hello@almondemberkitchen.com
  • Contact form: almondemberkitchen.com/contact
  • Location: Portland, Oregon, USA

This Privacy Policy was last reviewed and updated on March 1, 2025. If you have any questions about how we handle your data, don't hesitate to reach out — we're real people who care about your privacy.
