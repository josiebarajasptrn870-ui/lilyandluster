Quick answers

Can I republish a recipe? +
We're happy for you to share a link and a short excerpt, but please don't republish full recipes without written permission. See our Terms for details.
Do you accept sponsored posts? +
Occasionally, if the brand is a genuine fit with our kitchen values. Send us an email with "Collaboration" in the subject line and we'll review it.
I'm having trouble with a recipe. +
Please include the recipe name, your altitude, and any substitutions you made. That helps us troubleshoot much faster.
