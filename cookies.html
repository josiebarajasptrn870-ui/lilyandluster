Cookies Policy

We use cookies to make your visit to Almond Ember Kitchen smoother, more personal, and to understand which recipes bring you back for seconds.

Last updated: March 1, 2025

This Cookies Policy explains how Almond Ember Kitchen ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on www.almondemberkitchen.com (the "Site"). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal information more broadly.

By continuing to browse the Site, you agree to our use of cookies as described in this policy. If you do not agree, you can disable cookies in your browser settings (see Section 5), though some parts of the Site may not work as intended.

🍪 The short version: We use a few essential cookies to make the Site work, analytics cookies to see which recipes are popular, and preference cookies to remember your choices. We never use cookies to sell your data. You can manage or delete cookies at any time through your browser.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, to provide information to site owners, and to improve your browsing experience.

Cookies can be:

  • Session cookies — temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies — cookies that remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them.
  • First-party cookies — set by the website you are visiting (in this case, Almond Ember Kitchen).
  • Third-party cookies — set by a domain other than the one you are visiting (e.g., analytics providers).

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential functionality — to ensure the Site loads correctly and securely, and to remember your cookie preferences.
  • Analytics & performance — to understand how visitors interact with the Site, which pages and recipes are most popular, and how we can improve.
  • Preferences & personalization — to remember settings such as your language or region, and to provide a more tailored experience.
  • Marketing & social media — where applicable, to measure the effectiveness of our content and allow sharing via social platforms. We do not use invasive advertising cookies.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our Site are placed by third-party services we use to enhance functionality and analyze traffic. These third parties may include:

  • Google Analytics — for anonymized traffic analysis. You can learn more and opt out via Google Analytics Opt-out.
  • Social media platforms (e.g., Pinterest, Instagram) — when you interact with embedded content or share buttons.
  • Email service providers — for newsletter subscription management (only if you opt in).

We do not control these third-party cookies. We recommend reviewing the privacy and cookie policies of those providers for more information.

5. How to Manage Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by:

  • Browser settings — Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. The help section of your browser will guide you:
    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Safari
    • Microsoft Edge
  • Opt-out tools — For analytics cookies, you can use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Consent banner — Where required by law, we present a cookie consent banner when you first visit. You can change your choices at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the Site, or by using the settings link in the footer (if available).

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality and performance of the Site. Essential cookies cannot be disabled through our consent mechanism because they are necessary for the Site to operate.

6. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. Because there is no industry consensus on how to interpret DNT signals, our Site does not currently respond to DNT requests. We continue to monitor developments in this area.
